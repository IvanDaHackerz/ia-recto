## Implemented behavior recorded

**Source highlight and explanation.** `GeneratedExplanation.tsx` reveals words over at most five seconds, allows Show full answer, supplies complete screen-reader text, and completes for reduced motion or a hidden document. `LiveResult.tsx` displays a generated explanation only with citation and automated-support flags, or an explicitly legally verified record with its required flag; unchecked/provider-failed drafts remain withheld with usable local evidence. `ChatPage.tsx` matches the citation's chunk and optional concern/component to the selected response, opens/focuses the corresponding source block, and restores the invoking focus on mobile return. `LiveCitationCard` keeps `parent_text` complete; `markedSource` merges matching quote intervals and wraps them in `<mark>`. It highlights the first exact occurrence of each supplied quote and does not invent absent spans. Recorded dates, ranking “not confidence,” use restrictions and unreviewed current applicability remain separate from the highlight.

**Privacy and wording.** `PrivacyControls.tsx` names external AI use and encrypted-history opt-in. `SettingsContext.tsx` defaults AI on and saving off; those preferences and reading options persist in browser local storage when available. `backend/application/privacy.py` masks known credentials, contact, account, name and address patterns on a best-effort basis and explicitly sets `guaranteed_complete` false. The service checks local redacted wording before optional conditional interpretation; an accepted effective wording can trigger a second classifier/retrieval pass and is shown in the interface. Original wording remains in the local packet except recognized credentials are masked there too. Each submission remains independent. Together receives enabled-stage redacted question/source/draft inputs; masking does not establish anonymity or provider retention guarantees.

**History.** `SavedHistoryPage.tsx`, `App.tsx`, `ChatPage.tsx` and `backend/application/history.py` implement opt-in saved live packets, the `/saved/:savedId` reopen route, JSON export, single/all deletion confirmation and unavailable/empty/loading/error states. The store encrypts payloads with Windows-user DPAPI, scopes records by browser-session ownership, prunes at 30 days and caps retained turns. The cookie-signing secret is also DPAPI-protected in ignored `.local/website`; these are local browser/device controls rather than deployed account authentication. Opening a stored response does not rerun source checks. Scripted `/chat/:conversationId` examples remain in a separate archived disclosure.

**Metrics.** `PerformancePage.tsx` and `src/data/performance.ts` compute overview/detail scores from saved measured predictions with language/variation/path controls; withheld labels stay in the relevant expected-label denominator. Matrices include an abstain column and counts/row percentages, case inspection, class scores, routing and confidence calibration; filtered JSON export carries the selected filters/results. The separate full application replay does not follow those filters. The supplied current review records 436 reused synthetic inputs, 82 explanations shown, 354 withheld and 459 provider attempts. Availability, latency and estimated cost are operational measures, not independent legal-answer accuracy. The page says independent human and legal evaluation remain unestablished.
