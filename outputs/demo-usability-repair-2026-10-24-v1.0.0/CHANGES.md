# Demo usability repair — 24 October 2026

This is an engineering repair informed by previously inspected synthetic questions. It is not independent human validation or a legal accuracy study. Earlier assessments remain unchanged.

Changes:

- Retain ordinary scenario follow-ups and affected-worker references with their factual setup, while preserving new actors, unknown disputes and separate excluded concerns.
- Keep exclusive prediction/current-rate requests together with their context; a negated alternative does not become a positive supported concern.
- Preserve overseas salary context and recognize common employment, purchased-product and bank-account paraphrases.
- Protect ordinary English inflections and agent nouns from speculative space insertion, including contractor and production.
- Prefer authority about the requested issue: overtime offsetting, religious rest-day preference, payroll contents, deductions, project/seasonal status, platform redress, price labels and cash change. Exclude incidental deceased-worker, arbitration, training-tax and specialized homework provisions from unrelated requests.
- Give the live AI support assessor each component's actual query and requested focus. Require separate checks for answering the issue, actor identity and correct attachment of conditions. A failed check cannot authorize display even if the paragraph quotes a real source.
