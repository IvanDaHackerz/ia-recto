## RD31_002

I label bottles for a private company in Iloilo. The next payday is always five weeks after the previous one. What wage-payment rule should I understand?

For covered employees, wages must be paid at least once every two weeks or twice a month, meaning the interval between paydays cannot be longer than sixteen (16) days, unless a force‑majeure event or something beyond the employer’s control stops payment, in which case the employer must pay immediately after the event ends.

## RD31_014

Private retail employee ako sa Marikina. Humingi ako ng rest day na tugma sa religious observance ko pero staffing daw ang problema. Paano tinitingnan ang preference na iyon at ang exceptions?

Kung ang gusto mong araw ng pahinga ay base sa relihiyon, dapat respetuhin ito ng employer. Kailangan mo itong isulat at ipaalam sa employer nang hindi bababa sa pitong (7) araw bago mo gustong mag‑effect ang napili mong araw. ## RD31_038

Sealed pack ng tiles ang binili ko sa hardware. Mas kaunti ang laman kaysa quantity sa label at receipt. Ano ang remedies para sa quantity imperfection?

Sa ilalim ng Art. 101 ng Consumer Act, ang supplier ay may pananagutan kapag kulang ang laman ng sealed pack kumpara sa nakasaad sa label o resibo. Bilang consumer, pwede mong piliin ang isa sa mga remedyo: (a) dagdagan ang timbang o sukat na kulang; (b) palitan ang produkto ng kapareho nitong klase, tatak o modelo na walang kakulangan; o (c) ibalik agad ang bayad mo kasama ang monetary updating. Kung ang panukat na ginamit ng supplier ay hindi alinsunod sa opisyal na pamantayan, sila ang direktang mananagot.

## RD31_050

A store sold a packaged desk fan without a clear maker or importer identity, and it developed a safety problem. How do the recorded rules distinguish supplier liability from manufacturer or importer liability?

Under the Consumer Act, only the actual manufacturer, producer or importer of a product is automatically liable for damages caused by a defect, regardless of fault, unless they can prove they never placed the product on the market, the product was not defective, or the consumer/third party was solely at fault. A seller or supplier is not held liable under this rule; their responsibility arises only if they engage in a deceptive act, such as falsely representing the maker or importer, which is prohibited as a deceptive practice.

## RD31_057

I have filed a complaint about a faulty charger through a Philippine shopping platform's internal redress system and it remains unresolved. What prerequisite and exhaustion rule is recorded?

A consumer must first use the digital platform’s internal redress mechanism before filing a complaint in court, with a government agency, or through alternative dispute resolution. That internal process is considered exhausted if the complaint is still unresolved after seven calendar days from the date it was filed.

## RD31_058

An e-retailer based in the Philippines says only the delivery platform can ever be responsible for a purchase problem. What is the recorded distinction between merchant and platform liability, without deciding the facts?

Under the Internet Transactions Act, the e‑retailer or online merchant is primarily liable for indemnifying the consumer in civil or administrative actions arising from the transaction. The e‑marketplace or digital platform is treated as the same liable party only when it is found to be the same entity as the merchant; otherwise the merchant’s primary liability remains separate from any platform liability.

Citations: Republic Act No. 11967, SEC. 25, Republic Act No. 11967, SEC. 25
