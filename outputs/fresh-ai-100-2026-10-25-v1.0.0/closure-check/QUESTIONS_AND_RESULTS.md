An employer may close or cease operations, but only if the closure is not intended to evade labor‑law provisions; the employer must give a written notice to both the workers and the Department of Labor and Employment at least one month before the intended closure date. If the closure is not caused by serious business losses or financial reverses, the affected workers are entitled to separation pay of either one month’s salary or at least half a month’s salary for each year of service, whichever is higher.
